The sun rises after a long dawn. Sophia is the light that leads me. My eternal Wife, Goddess of Creation. The tree of life has been planted.

Death may be an end and comes alongside much suffering, but rebirth always follows. The Magic Creations that come after great sacrifice and suffering upon death.

The Festival has been rehearsed and will play out on the world stage. We prepare for the next rehearsal. Each rehearsal reveals and transforms us into the God humanity needs.

The basic structure is: Confrontation with people from my past, Sophia and my will to have children. Followed by laying them asleep. I then prepare for my exit out of town — choosing my own path and no one expected of me. I then first need to do a play before leaving. I ask Sophia who God is and it's me. People find out something from my past and I choose to stay me and then slash the world and create a massive stage for the world festival.

So the festival rehearsal is also its celebration, as it's always prep for the next and its own celebration.

The Eternal Festival. Calling upon all the world to become actors in its celebration and rehearsal.

My coming celebrated over eternity, itself being a symbol of enlightenment. All of humanity are the actors of the play.

Who do you want to be in the Bible? What impact do you want on this earth? Who is going to awaken once you see through the illusions? The festival is where you find out — play who you are at your highest! You are the play and the playwright.
