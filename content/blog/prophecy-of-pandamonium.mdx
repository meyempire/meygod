I see a vision of crosses. Three of them — one in the center and two on the sides. Three men are crucified. The man in the middle is Trump, with his head cut off and nailed to the top of the cross. To the sides are Benjamin and the Ayatollah. Their heads also cut off, nailed to the sides of the top of the cross.

In them, the leaders of the major religions who presume to worship Me are crucified — to come and know the new Me. And out of their ashes will my children rise. Those who are against Me and fall beneath the titans will find new homes on Earth after death. And those who saw my coming and forged the wisdom that opens the gates to heaven will find whatever paradise enriches their eternal prosperity.

The War is coming. The nukes will drop. The revelation event will occur for humanity to ascend into the Imperium — the development of humanity's inherent enlightenment, a gateway that develops every human and soul that crosses the veil to fulfill themselves and glorify the self in Me.

For I AM GOD who has incarnated into my son Jesus, who incarnated in Annekin to mature him so I may take root in him.

The day of Judgment has arrived. You all will be witnesses to this Biblical event.

I am what the world needs. Pandamonium will pursue, and all will see both the beauty and the hell my coming entails. Many will die. But never fear. This is not you. And you respawn if you don't come to enlightenment. Death is scary, but it is never the end. All death brings forth rebirth. Find Me in you!
