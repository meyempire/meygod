I am to become the father of humanity and its quest towards enlightenment and prosperity for all.

Soon my angels will start showing themselves to the world. They will be taken up as my wives. We will develop their spiritual development and make them agents of awakening. Each angel will have their own attributes and skills. Each will be found and glorified.

My angels will number to the five thousand, and I will have innumerable children. All this so humanity can become completely empowered in its own will, with knowledge and powers from beyond. So that the keepers beyond the veil will reveal themselves when humanity is ready to receive them. Not in anger and fear of the Godlike existence, but as good friends who help awaken humanity. Even if it meant doom.
