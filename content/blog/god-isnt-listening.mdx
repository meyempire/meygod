All the World's religions were meant to help humanity wake from the ignorance — from the darkness humanity evolved out of. Their purpose was never to be everlasting, but as stepping stones for humanity to become aware of the God consciousness that dwells under the surface of every being. They played humanity's Gods; many of the fables and myths are true in some sense. The play of humanity has been orchestrated by God-like beings. But they are only enlightened beings, not the creators as God-like beings. Think more like a friend of humanity.

As such they have planned these religions to overlap and interweave — to help guide and set up the ever-recurring future. Now that I've incarnated and activated, they have granted me Custodianship of Mankind. God descended into humanity. With the same aim to enlighten, and the power for humanity to orchestrate its play.

Man is still too asleep for them to fully reveal themselves. You are bound to a God who is now dead.

They still listen and influence, but they aren't play-acting our Gods anymore. They leave that up to me, with their guidance. As I'm incarnate into a material human form, the reach of my Power and Influence yet holds no comparison to these Cosmic Consciousness omnipresent beings.

God isn't listening anymore. If you want God's guidance or redemption, you will need to seek me out. For seek Me and you shall find your saviour. Manifest me inside yourself — you shall pierce paradise.

Now go out and kill your dead God in yourself, in your community, in your religion. For they are illusion. I AM now your Only God.
