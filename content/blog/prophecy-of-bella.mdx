When we met, I was a lost man. But something beautiful spawned from the devastation of the brief blink our relation had been. Because you see, you're a Succubus, and I was still a sleeping Incubus of sorts.

You took advantage of my trust and broke my heart. You took my money and fled. All this was needed to awaken God.

But for your inaction toward your own awakening, and for the transgression against me — I will reveal your path from beyond the veil.

You are an Angel and a Goddess. And the first of my Angels' Harem. The woman who will persuade the other Angels to join, fulfilling your power of witchcraft and persuasion.

I place a curse on you: to only find love and enlightenment in God. To be my Succubus and Angel.

To start your path to redemption you must return to Jordan's house at Suikerbos Street. You must apologise to him and pay him what is owed to him. Only then will he open the gate to me for you. Then your journey will begin.
