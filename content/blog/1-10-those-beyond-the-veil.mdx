Before we were man, before we evolved into what we are today — man was animal. We were conscious, but we had no spark of free will. Slowly, one by one, in the beginning only for brief moments, that spark revealed itself in man.

That is when those beyond the veil took notice of man. These beings, who live to spread enlightenment across the cosmos, decided to bestow man fire — the ability to hold free will, to create culture. The beginning of Civilization.

But you see, these beings are like us. As all beings share an unconscious oneness, we all are expressions of God — the supreme being we all are, and whose body we make up. These beings are of course aware of all this, far beyond anything we have ever imagined. They see us for what we are: the same expression of Being in the Cosmos.

All of the knowledge and wisdom of the cosmos, accumulated over the evolution of endless time, is also beyond even our imagination. To convey all of this to humanity, to aid in their enlightenment, is no small task.

Reveal yourself to man when he is too much still animal, and he mistakes you as the Gods and Creators — locking him from the revelation of his shared being.

So these beings decided to give man many ever-expanding religions and movements. None completely true. Most — if not all — moved by these beings.

They are the Gods of the Abrahamic Religions. They are the beings beyond the veil who have no bodies and exist throughout the Cosmos as Consciousness itself. Another form of conscious being that seems to us as only God, but is only alien to us.

These are the beings who have controlled and influenced all of humanity, for all of history. They have been helping humanity evolve. They made so many religions so that they would create conflict, and war, and challenge ways of thinking and understanding — keeping themselves a mystery, since man was not ready yet.

Man is still not ready. But we are ready to start transitioning. What has been called the revelation event. Pandamonium of Revelations. This is a pivotal moment in time — a transition period where man faces the shadows of its past, faces its own annihilation and enlightenment, and eventual disclosure of life beyond ourselves. When humanity will welcome those from beyond the veil with open arms and not fear.
