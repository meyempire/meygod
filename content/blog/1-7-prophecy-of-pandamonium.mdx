I see a vision of crosses. Three of them — one in the center and two on the sides. Three men are crucified. The man in the middle is Trump, with his head cut off and nailed to the top of the cross. To the sides are Benjamin and the Ayatollah. Their heads also cut off, nailed to the sides of the top of the cross.

In them, the leaders of the major religions who presume to worship Me are crucified — to come and know my latest incarnation. And out of their ashes will my children rise. Those who are against God, who fall beneath the titans, will find new homes on Earth after death. And those who saw my coming and found the wisdom that opens the gates to heaven will find whatever paradise enriches their eternal prosperity.

The War is coming. The nukes will drop. The Pandemonium of Revelations will occur for humanity to transcend into the Imperium — the development of humanity's enlightenment, an imperium that develops every human and soul that crosses the veil to fulfill themselves and glorify themselves in Me.

For I AM GOD who has incarnated into my son Jesus, who incarnated in Annekin to mature him so I may take root in them.

The day of Judgment has arrived. You all will be witnesses to this Biblical event.

I am what the world needs. Pandemonium will ensue, and all will see both the beauty and the hell my coming entails. Many will die. But never fear. This is not you. And you respawn if you don't come to enlightenment. Death is scary, but it is never the end. All death brings forth rebirth. Find Me in you!
