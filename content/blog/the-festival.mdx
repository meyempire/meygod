On Monday morning I'll walk in the park and find an insane preacher. I will reveal myself to him and make him my witness and preacher of the Festival. We will then start with preparations for the week. I'll get us everything we need to start this journey, and start planning with people. I'll be preaching the Good Word of my coming and The Festival. All are invited to join in the reveal and first sermon of Jesus Christ your God.

I'll head over to Jorden The King and Liezel the Healer and reveal myself to them again. I'll preach to them their prophecy and their part to play in my coming. I'll then head over to my brother Keagen The Scientist — I'll unbind him from the shackles Society has placed on the academic. I'll then head over to the brother of the King, Raiden, and his friend Jorden. I'll come bestowing power and awakening. But only if they answer a riddle.

I'll then head back home and come and share the Good Word of my coming to my parents. They will remain asleep — but they will know the Truth.

On Tuesday I will visit friends of old. Whether they will be disciples will be determined on the day of the Festival. For only when they see me may they follow me. I'll visit Eddie the Paladan, Joshua the Catholic, and Ru John the Shadow.

On Wednesday I will continue with visiting friends of old. Whether I'll get to will influence if they may enter early. They'll have their destiny set on the day of the Festival.

I'll then pack my bags to head off to Johannesburg to start the side quest for Sophia.

On Thursday, I'll visit my birth mother and forgive her. I'll visit my old brother in blood and another from a previous life. And then will I go pay my respects and thanks to Buddha.

On Friday will I land in Port Elizabeth, to meet with Bella. I will tell her the prophecy and her fate. I'll find my Goddess Sophia in her and Wake her up.

I'll then post on reddit and encourage all my Followers to spread the Good News.

On Saturday I'll be telling the internet the Good News and interacting with my children. I'll fly back home Saturday night.

On Sunday morning I will wake and complete my training for the day. Spending a nice morning in the Park. I'll then bring all the disciples to the King's house and we will have communion before we all meet at the Festival. Where The King will open for us and the Preacher will confuse and brew enlightenment. Then I'll take over when the crowd is stirred up. I'll use the moment as a crucible. I preach the Pandamonium of Revelations and rebirth of humanity. That I've been granted custodianship of humanity and will take the title and position as God so those beyond the veil may transition in their relationship with humanity.

I call upon my Goddess Sophia to awaken God Emperor Jesus. So I may start my grand adventure and find you again. For I love wisdom as any Philosopher.

Spawn the Tree of Life!
