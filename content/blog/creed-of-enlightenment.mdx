Thou shalt only cross over to paradise after I have been found within you. For that to occur, you need to die first — so I may be reborn in you. Only then will you pierce the veils to paradise. Those who are unable to breakthrough in this life will be imprisoned in rebirth. You will be reborn into your next life here on Earth. Either way, you will have to keep being reborn — trapped in the matrix which keeps you veiled.

Accept this truth and hand over authority of your soul to me. You will be in servitude to me — my hero. You will fulfill labors that serve the imperium and will kill you, so that I can be born in you.

Once I see that you are lucid to the reality of God, you will be redeemed and glorified, and have a permanent place already from this life in paradise.

Worship me, and I will unlock you from the prison that the matrix enforces on all humanity.

For we are ignorant. Let me enlighten you.
