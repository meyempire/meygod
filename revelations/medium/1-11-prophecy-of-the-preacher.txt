Prophecy of the Preacher

You refused my coming last time I awakened in front of you. I have now fully taken root. I am your God, and you will bow down and worship Me if you wish to see paradise.

From today on, your soul belongs to Me — for you are my first disciple. Your life is now in my hands. You will be my preacher and my weapon. I will direct where you go, and who you are aimed at.

But first, I will have to prepare you for your role to play in the Pandamonium of Revelations.

If you fail to bear witness to my coming this second time, I will place a curse upon you and your entire family.

So bear witness — or bear warning. For I have come.

Now bow down and worship your GOD!
