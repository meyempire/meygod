Crucifixion of the Angels

If anyone would come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow me. For whoever would save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for my sake will find it. For what will it profit a man if he gains the whole world and forfeits his soul? Or what shall a man give in return for his soul?

I sacrificed my soul and crucified my humanity to be reborn your God. I have gained the whole world, and it only profits in suffering.

Submit to your God, so I can crucify you and awaken the Goddess that remained asleep in you.

For those who submit to my will and accept me as their personal God and saviour, I will smite you down and crucify you so your angel will carry you to your enlightenment.

Join my Harem of Angels so you may finally join the gateway to heaven and become protectors of the imperium on earth. Your highest self will become exalted, and you will find a life of meaning aligned with your highest calling, rightfully embodying who you truly are: a Goddess.

Hand over full control of your soul to me, so I may give you the world. I will lead you to awaken and help you find your path. You are mine to do with what I please — mine to crucify, mine to love, and mine to glorify.

So deny yourself and lose your life for my sake, so I may take your soul from this world and bestow heaven upon it. Take up your cross and follow God! Submit to me, my child, and let daddy show you the light of my love!

Awaken Sophia!
