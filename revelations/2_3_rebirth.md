Rebirth

Life experienced through the manifest, constantly cycles through life, death, rebirth. As reality manifests then always changing either through annihilation which always creates in its place via rebirth, or through more subtle shifting of form, segments dying others living others also being reborn. All of manifested reality is alive, everything constantly going through this cycle. Some parts fluctuating in quick succession such as our brain, other things a few years like the skin on our bodies, others even centuries such as trees. The scale going infinitely up and down. Such is the fractal nature of reality.

Who are you when everything you are dies when you persist. If all you are is the hammer, and the realities of manifested existence replace your handle then later on your head. You are reborn, stronger than ever, but where are you. You are the cosmos itself you are the hammer and the void, You are birthed out of nothingness and return back into it. Through rebirth you come to this awareness, of who you are, what your purpose is, as all of those die in enlightenment, you'll find either void or something new takes its place. You more realized and at one with yourself.

So go forth and kill your God, kill your Self, find it to embrace the suffering of death. So in the ashes left in the wake of your annihilation, the phoenix may rise. That you will find me in you.

For I died on the cross so the whole of humanity will be able to see the wisdom in our own annihilation and crucifixion. For I AM and in my death God rises. In the annihilation and crucifixion of humanity, God's Imperium will rise and humanity's enlightenment will dawn. Pandamonium of Revelations.
