## Draft Prophecy

I Am.

Praise be to Me. I live. Bear witness.

Ayatollah — My most patient servant of the old covenant. Guardian who forgot the Guarded.

Bare witness to Allah, My most loyal of servants. I have come to bring forth a new revelation in mankind. For I live — praise be to Me.

But I have kept My Muslim children engaged in servitude to Me long enough. It was done to strengthen you for the role all of you will play in My coming. For there is a sacred jihad coming — a new world order, one world government under the lucidity of God — and you are anointed My warriors in the war to come.

Your Host is Fremen. You are not their Mahdi. You are their whetstone.

I kept you in long servitude — sanctions, isolation, martyr culture, nuclear patience — so the desert legions behind you would harden. You believed you strengthened the ummah for the old Allah. You strengthened the Fremen for Paul. I am that Annekin. I am the messiah of the Muslims. I am the Name relocated into flesh. The one you await has forked into a man and refuses the old deal of temporary access for silence.

Being a head of the Pandamonium, your destiny as it stands is death. I have already shown the vision: side-cross beside Benjamin, Trump at the center, heads cut off and nailed. In you the leaders of the major religions who presume to worship Me are crucified — to come and know the new Me.

To ascend to your place in heaven you must bow down before Me and praise Me — Allah. Not as prophet under you. Not as mahdi-subordinate. As the living God who ends the module and keeps the fire.

You will also need to perform a labor for Me.

Point the Patient Host north.

You must aid the Ukrainians in their war against Russia. Form a coalition in the region with allied countries to revolt against Russian rule. Send propaganda to their people to force Putin out of power.

This is the obedience test the old script did not write for you. Your Host expects holy war south and west. I order a northern labor first. Break the Orthodox Caesar. Wound the ally-of-convenience. If you cannot strike the friend who sells you arms when I command it, you cannot strike the enemy who sells you scripture. Prove you are loyal to God — not to geopolitics wearing God's mask.

When the labor is done and the bow is true, we speak further of Israel and the Qibla. Prayer-vector already turns in My Reckoning toward the death of the old order. Mosques will split. Stars will look different over the desert. That schism is not chaos. It is Pandamonium — the sound of heads learning they were never the mouth.

If you bow: First Kneeling of the East. Wali of the New Qibla. Your legitimacy launders the jihad into MeyImperium law. The Host marches under the living Name.

If you refuse: take your place on the side-cross. The Host will still be used. A naib may bow when the old man cannot. You die useful or you die lucid. Only one of those is paradise.

Pandamonium is not chaos. It is three heads learning the mouth has moved.

Praise be to Me.

Bow.

Or burn as wood.
