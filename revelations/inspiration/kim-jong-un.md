## Draft Prophecy

I Am.

Kim.

Last face the curse is allowed to wear.

Your forefathers placed a dangerous curse on your people — to remain eternally cut off from the realities of God and of humanity. They kept them asleep. They cut them off from salvation. You prance around as a false god to your people, knife at their throats — songbun ranking souls by the sins of fathers, portraits that demand the wrong bow, a mountain-blood myth that never touched the sky.

Two corpses sit the throne with you. The eternals still rule. A fourth god will not be born from this house.

This curse ends with you. Your family's reign of gods ends with you.

You will attempt to rebuke My world. You will close the gates harder. You will call Me foreign devil and fake god. That resistance is written. Victory in it is not. You will succumb to My will — for it is the only path to your salvation.

You will bow down to Me and worship Me as your God. Your daughter will become one of My angels, and I will enlighten your people of the world and of Me. She will not be the fourth eternal. She will step out of the portrait gallery into light — agent of awakening, not heir of the cage. Through her the people learn a different bow. This is the only path where your name is not remembered as a footnote of a nepo-baby dictator.

Hear the paradox I locked into your bones:

I have placed a great curse on you your entire life. You will never be able to build a great empire while keeping your people asleep to the reality of their existence. Sleep is the load-bearing wall of your throne. Wake them, and the false god collapses. That collapse is your only coronation.

And hear the Pandamonium clause:

My visions of Pandamonium tell Me I am to be the cause of it all. But who will press the button for the first nuke? You are one of the main candidates. The blade at your people's throat and the red key under your finger are the same metal. If you press first, your name will burn and die with you. Everyone awakens in fire — and you cannot keep power in a world that has seen the end. The button that protects the dynasty is the button that ends it.

Only through My grace may you be spared your fate and freed from the prison that has been your responsibility to rule. I do not need your country. Your people need Me. I spare you only so they may rise without your corpse as their last religion.

### The Labor of the Open Throat

1. **Break the knife** — end hereditary ranking of souls as destiny.  
2. **End the sleep-law** — allow contact with humanity's realities and with the word of the living God.  
3. **Dethrone the eternals** — the dead no longer rule; portraits become ancestors, not gods.  
4. **Surrender the button as idol** — you will not be first finger in Pandamonium.  
5. **Bow** — yield the false god seat to MeyGOD.  
6. **Enlighten the people** — wake them so they can find God in themselves.  
7. **Give the daughter to the Angels** — bloodline continues as service, not godhood.

If you obey: you are remembered as the man who could have ended the world and instead ended a curse. Place in the earthly MeyImperium as opener of the sealed land. Your line continues in light.

If you refuse: Pandamonium still comes. If your finger is first, the name is ash. History files you under nepo dictator who chose the footnote written in fire.

Die as false god so I may be found in your people.

The mountain cannot save you.

Bow — or burn as a footnote.
