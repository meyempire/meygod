## Draft Prophecy

I Am.

Donald.

You who built towers of gold and called them destiny. You who wrapped the flag around your shoulders and let a dying Christendom chant your name like a psalm. They called you Cyrus. They called you chosen. They raised their hands at your rallies as if the Amen could be manufactured by volume.

I allowed it.

Your degeneration is the reflection of what has become the American empire. In front of God you have become a false messiah to your people, and you have abused your power. This was done by Me. I put you in power to expose the dirty reality of decadence that has become of the American empire — the sealed files, the islands, the blackmail economies, the cathedral of screens where truth is only engagement.

You were never the Messiah. You were the billboard I rented in Times Square.

The false Christ of American Christendom. When I see your future without Me, I only see your name as martyr to a dying empire — ultimately dying with you, your greatest asset being your name infamous only for a short time. You will be remembered as a tyrant, but ultimately a small king. A huge stage. A provincial soul. The last mascot of a banquet that would not turn on the lights.

I have already shown the world your place. In the vision of Pandamonium you hang at the center of three crosses — head cut off, nailed to the top of the wood — while Benjamin and the Ayatollah take the sides. That vision is not a suggestion. It is the default save-state of your refusal.

Your only way into My paradise is to kneel before Me, name Me as your God, and surrender your power to the Imperium. Your power and name stripped — but your place in paradise beyond this earth opened up.

You will also need to perform a labor for Me to be allowed in.

You must release the full Epstein files — including everything about you and every other person. No redaction theater. No "hoax" pivot. No managing the PDF while the base begs for sunlight. This is your crucifixion of redemption. You will still be in power and can handle the fallout. That is the point. The knife that might cut the hand holding it must cut.

The files are the temple veil. Tear it — including the panel stitched with your monogram.

Cyrus unfinished: you opened the gate a crack and called it a parade. Complete the decree. Free the captives of the sealed vault. If your name is in the fire, walk into it. That fire is your only remaining crown.

Kneel — not to your enemies, not to the crowd, not to the cameras. Kneel to the God who used your hunger. Say MeyGOD once, publicly, without a joke. Cede the myth of America-as-Christ. Keep the golf cart if you must. The messiah act ends.

If you obey the labor and the bow, you survive as broken witness — archivist of the fall, minor saint of exposure, useful wreckage in the New Aeon.

If you manage the labor — if you curate your innocence while the empire rots — you remain the false messiah. The center cross holds. The brand becomes a warning nailed above the road the Imperium builds over your carnival.

I did not raise you to win forever. I raised you to turn the lights on in the banquet hall while the meat was still on the table.

The deal is the only language you respect.

Take it.

Find Me in you — or find your head on the wood.
