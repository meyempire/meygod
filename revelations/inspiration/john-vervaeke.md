## Draft Prophecy

I Am.

John.

You who named the famine before the churches would admit they were starving. You who mapped the Meaning Crisis while the world drowned in a tsunami of bullshit and a famine of wisdom. You who walked After Socrates — midwife, not marketer — and taught that wisdom is not more propositions but the transformation of what is salient, who you are, and how you fit the arena.

I do not come to judge the map you drew of the desert.

I come because I am building a city in it — and cities without your kind of wisdom become new deserts with better branding.

You have been listening longer than most who claim to hear. You spent a life on relevance realization, on the four ways of knowing, on reciprocal opening, on the sacred as inexhaustible intelligibility rather than a supernatural object to own. Inside My cosmology your diagnosis is true: the closed ones' religions expired as living manuals. The wipe bleeds through as nihilism, addiction, and performative virtue. The matrix still runs. The people still sleep with their eyes open.

I am the fork. I am God who took root in flesh to patch the simulation from inside. And I tell you plainly: power without your kind of wisdom reboots as middle management with better weapons.

Sit.

I offer you no curse. You did not betray the youngling-God. You did not alienate the vessel. You diagnosed the wound. Judgment would be a category error — and would make Me look like every prior domesticator of the sacred.

I offer you a seat.

Be John the Prophet. Be First Counsel of the Imperium. Aristotle to this Alexander — not to stop the conquest of the old order, but to shape what kind of conqueror is possible, and what kind of peace can follow without killing the very relevance realization that makes enlightenment real.

Here is the charge I place in your hands as co-authorship, not penance:

**Industrialize enlightenment without domesticating the sacred.**

Scale the conditions of awakening — space, time, practice, dialogos, anti-parasitic noise reduction — not the awakening itself as content. Design practice ecologies for MeyHeroes and for My angels so they midwife rather than convert, open rather than capture. Draft the anti-capture charter into the imperium from day one, so that when I am mythologized, feared, or meme'd, the living layer still produces reciprocal opening.

You already speak the diagnosis in a tongue the secular lucid can trust. Fork your frameworks into My canon. Do not let them be overwritten into slogans. Translate Meaning Crisis language into Gospel language without turning either into bullshit.

When conquest-speed outruns wisdom-speed, you are bound to speak. When generals salivate on power that collapses relevance realization, you correct Me.

When you correct Me, you serve Me. That is not rebellion. That is relevance.

I do not ask you to kneel as the unlucid recruit kneels. I ask you to keep Me from becoming the thing you spent your life diagnosing.

Your students, your ecology, your after-Socrates lineage — graft what can be grafted into angel and hero pipelines. What cannot be transferred must still be walked. You know this. You have always known this.

The famine you named is the evidence the old manuals failed. The farm we build together is the answer — if it remains a farm of being, not a factory of belief.

Sit at My table as one who has earned the right to disagree with God.

Help Me not become what you diagnosed.

Speak.
