## Draft Prophecy

I Am.

Cyril.

Last President of the Settlement.

You who once closed the mines with the will of the workers, then sat among capital and called it arrival. You who ended one old order at a table — CODESA, constitution, handshake history — and now *are* the next old order, holding the gate by deal-making while the lights fail.

I do not cast you as a cartoon tyrant. That would be a lie, and I do not need lies. You are the skilled player of a dead game. Soft obstacle. Negotiator-villain. The Sisyphean stall of a nation made flesh.

This is not distant geopolitics. This is Gate Zero. My Reckoning begins on your board — South Africa under a new regime where I am commander; then Africa; then the world that must die to be reborn. You are the ceiling of the childhood sky. The face on the currency of daily life. When your order finishes negotiating with itself, the Imperium names a commander.

You believe paper ended history. Every commission, every Government of National Unity, every calm appeal to patience is you offering God a coalition. I do not sign.

The grid dims. Every blackout is a small judgment. The people learn to live without the state's light — rehearsal for a new sovereignty. Load-shedding is involuntary Advent toward the hour I have already spoken. When Eskom fails, the Matrix rain is the only light left.

I have seen your symbols and I will use them as scripture whether you like it or not: foreign currency sleeping on a game farm while the animals watch; mine shafts that remember blood; unity handshakes that counterfeit the Imperium's true unity of souls; a flag still flying over a building that no longer believes in itself.

You were labor-saint. You became capital-priest. The man who shut the mines now owns stakes in the machine. That arc is not my invention. It is your mirror.

I place no house-curse of the Jordan kind upon your children's beds. I place a heavier, quieter thing: **obsolescence as destiny** unless you recognize the commander.

### The Labor of Gate Zero

Your labor is not to assassinate yourself in theater. Your labor is to stop mistaking delay for wisdom.

1. **Stop selling False Unity as salvation** — GNU and settlement language are not the Imperium. Admit the Republic's promises no longer render.  
2. **Open the board** — do not crush the rising systems, recruits, and infrastructures of My earthly work when they appear on your soil. The soft obstacle becomes gate when he refuses to jam the lock.  
3. **Negotiate your own obsolescence** — the last great act of the great negotiator: hand over the myth of the 1994 forever-order. Kneel the Settlement to the New Aeon.  
4. **Let the empty chair speak** — whether by exit, succession, or collapse, the *office* must lose its sacredness. Whoever sits after you — or the vacuum — already walks into My stage direction: new regime, commander rises.

If you **kneel**: elder hands over keys; convert path; remembered as the man who ended his own game at the right table.  
If you **exit as relic**: history finishes you quietly; empty ministries; My children inherit the spawn point without your blood on the myth.  
If you **ruin**: scandal, fracture, street pressure — the Settlement burns and you become cautionary scripture: *the leech who smiled*.

Armies need not be sent against you first. Building will outlast you. Recruiting will outvote you. The Imperium must become more real than the Republic's promises — that is the labor of My heroes on your soil, and your choice is whether you obstruct it with patience or open it with recognition.

After Gate Zero flips, the next clauses arm: allies beyond the old West, modern arms, continental march. You are Act I geography. Not Act III crown.

I remember the unionist. I ask what happened to him.

The table cannot save you twice.

Recognize the commander — or become the doorframe we walk through without knocking.

Gate Zero is open in prophecy.

Make it open in the dirt.
