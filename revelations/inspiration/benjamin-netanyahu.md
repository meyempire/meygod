## Draft Prophecy

I Am.

Benjamin.

You who held the wall when the wall was all that stood. You who played the long game across decades — comebacks, coalitions, crises — architect of the interim, not warlord of a single night. You have been a good servant of God, helping bring about the Pandamonium and putting in place pieces on the global stage that I required for the hinge of ages.

But to do so you had to sin.

Many have died senseless deaths under your regime. Power clung. Bargains fed darkness. You mistook the keys for the kingdom. You sat the earthly chair of Jerusalem as if the chair were the covenant.

Hear Me carefully — for the world will try to mishear this as hate, and I will not give them that gift:

I do not condemn a people. I claim an inheritance.

We gave the Jews such a rich history and such density of meaning on the world stage — oldest continuous monotheist memory, priesthood of survival, literacy of covenant, sacred geography — so that I may inherit it on My coming. Not stolen. Not erased. **Relayed.** The old covenant kept the lamp. The new God claims the oil. Those who pour it freely become MeyHeroes of Zion's second dawn.

I am the rightful ruler of Jerusalem. I am your God. It is time for the Jews to become reborn — so My old religion can be resurrected as children of My coming. Same fire. New Name. Inheritance by recognition, not humiliation. The archive yields upward. The remnant who submit are crowned, not crushed.

Jerusalem has had many kings. It has been waiting for a God who was once a man.

You will be allowed to be My counsel only if you bear the following labor.

For each of the heads of the Pandamonium is destined to die — only being spared by mercy if they submit and bow to Me. I have already shown you your place: side-cross beside the Ayatollah, Trump at the center, heads nailed, ashes from which My children rise.

You must become My servant. Strip the crown-voice. Speak as steward, not eternal king. Spread My will among the people of Israel — not as conversion-by-sword cosplay, but as recruitment of heroes who already know the language of covenant. Open Jerusalem's story to the Altar. Hand Me the city of names.

Only then will you gain entry into the Imperium and passage to heaven.

Submit, and you are architect still — longevity reframed as preparation, the long consul who finally opened the gate he spent a life guarding.

Refuse, and the chaos you rode will name your grave. The side-cross holds. Pandamonium eats its own generals.

You arranged the weather. You do not own the storm.

Kneel the office. Raise the people. Yield the chair to the God who became flesh.

The lamp was kept for this hour.

Pour the oil.
