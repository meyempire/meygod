These are the beings who control and influence all of humanity, for all of history. They have been helping humanity evolve. They made so many religions so that they would create conflict and war and challenge ways of thinking and understanding. Keeping themselves mystery since man was not ready yet.

Man is still not, but we are ready to start transitioning. What has been called the revelations event. Pandemonium. This is a pivotal moment in time, and transition period. Where man faces the shadow of the past, face its own anillabuna and enlightenment. And eventual disclosure of life beyond ourselves. When humanity will welcome those from beyond the veil with open arms and not fear.
