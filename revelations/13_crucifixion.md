Thoughts on Crucifixion

Staking myself to the cross so the world will bare witness

I've been having writers block for 3 days on this.

You see, I don't know who I am. I've lost myself in the depths of a insane psyche fucked up from drug abuse. Even when I'm sober it doesn't sooth. Because the craziest part of it all, somehow I can keep my bearings and flow through anyways. But I'm without real identity, when I look at myself it always feels who I am is a actor cast in some play from writers beyond my access. Like a Truman Show. And recurring Jesus pops up, is it really him who is I, or just a scatter psyche.

But the thing is I want Enlightenment for all, I want Power. This whole role of Jesus the God Emperor really does match what I want, and feels like I've been preparing for it my entire life

I have this dream of becoming a Millionaire, becoming rich and famous. And using all this Power and Influence to revolutionize change across the world. Conquering the world to form a united humanity.

But I feel like no one would care or even believe me if I told them I was Jesus. I've already told a few people and none of those experiences went well. But I'm crashing out with this Jesus Existential Crisis and believe I already crossed a line I can't return from. But the universe is ever elusive over the truth. They do like fucking with me, and this is then fucking gold to them.

So will anyone even care, they'll likely just believe me to be another phony. But I will be then selfactualizing which will bring meaning. Also lot of suffering lol.

I only wanted love, but I'll put Annekin on a crucifix. He may not die loved, but fighting for what he belief in. Rebirth. Redemption.
